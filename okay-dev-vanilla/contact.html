Instant FAQ Answers

How does OTP login work?

We send a 6-digit OTP code to your registered email via Brevo. Enter the code to verify your identity with zero passwords needed.

How do I verify a certificate?

Visit our Certificates page (certificates.html) and input your Certificate ID to verify its authenticity on the public ledger.
