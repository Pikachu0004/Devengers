COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Devengers, our community, events, learning resources, and campus initiatives.

Yes, 100% free! Devengers is a student and developer community built on open collaboration. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden costs to join our Discord/WhatsApp channels, participate in hackathons, access learning roadmaps, or collaborate with fellow builders.
We organize and partner on national college hackathons, technical workshops, coding bootcamps, open-source sprints, and placement prep webinars in collaboration with industry platforms like GitHub, Google, Unstop, Devfolio, MLH, and premier universities across India.
Learn is our curated computer science hub for B.Tech students. It includes verified free tracks in Full-Stack Web Development, Data Structures & Algorithms, AI/ML, and Core CS Systems, alongside GeeksforGeeks documentation roadmaps and downloadable PDF cheat sheets.
Campus Resources is our academic archive offering free, verified previous year question papers (PYQs), revision notes, lab manuals, and syllabus roadmaps across engineering universities (including AKTU, Mumbai University, GTU, VTU, and more) to boost your semester prep.
Every day, our team curates a handpicked algorithmic problem (from Arrays to Dynamic Programming and Graphs). You solve it, maintain your daily consistency streak, share your approach in the community discussion, and level up your problem-solving skills for technical interviews.
The Gallery showcases excellence across our ecosystem. We feature community projects, developer portfolios, national hackathon winners, and community leader spotlights with digital badges and priority referrals.
If you are a passionate student builder or tech club lead, you can start an official Devengers Chapter at your campus! We provide event sponsorship, mentor guidance, industry speakers, and promotion across our network. Contact us at support@devengers.org to get started.
